## Reporting & Court-Admissible Forensic Documentation

The framework generates formal, authoritative Digital Forensic Examination Reports adhering to international law enforcement and judicial admissibility standards, specifically:
- **ISO/IEC 27037:2012**: Guidelines for identification, collection, acquisition, and preservation of digital evidence.
- **ISO/IEC 27042:2015**: Guidelines for the analysis and interpretation of digital evidence.
- **NIST SP 800-86**: Guide to Integrating Forensic Techniques into Incident Response.
- **Federal Rules of Evidence (FRE) Rule 901**: Authenticating or Identifying Evidence.
- **Federal Rules of Evidence (FRE) Rule 902(13) & 902(14)**: Certified Records Generated by an Electronic Process or System (Self-Authenticating Evidence).
- **Daubert v. Merrell Dow Pharmaceuticals (509 U.S. 579)**: Scientific validity, peer review, known error rates, and deterministic repeatability.

### Mandatory Report Structure (12 Formal Sections):
1. **Administrative Case Record & Authority**: Official Case Reference ID (`CR-FOR-2026-{id}`), Exhibit Item Tracking (`EVD-IMG-{id}`), Requesting Authority, Matter Title, Lead Examiner (`Soyam Maji (Soyam08)`), Ingestion and Examination Timestamps (UTC).
2. **Forensic Chain of Custody & Dual-Hash Verification**: Corroborated dual cryptographic hashing (MD5 + SHA-256) calculated at intake; bitstream integrity verification (0-bit deviation); 4-stage chain of custody movement log (Intake -> Working Copy Replicated -> Non-Destructive Analysis -> Vault Preserved).
3. **Validated Forensic Tooling Registry (Daubert Standard)**: Deterministic software registry specifying tools, versions, and peer-reviewed algorithmic basis (Pillow v11.x, OpenCV v4.14.0, NumPy/SciPy 2D FFT/DCT, ForensicSpatialFrequencyNet-v1, Grad-CAM).
4. **Physical & Logical File Specifications**: Container format, MIME, byte size, resolution, megapixels, aspect ratio, color mode, bit depth, chroma subsampling (SOF0), quantization quality (DQT ~94%), encoding process, and pixel density.
5. **Timeline & Temporal Provenance (FRE 901)**: Original creation timestamp, modification timestamp, forensic intake timestamp, and temporal consistency discrepancy determination.
6. **Optical Sensor Footprint & Exposure Parameters**: Camera make/model, lens model, shutter speed, aperture (f-number), ISO gain, focal length, flash state, white balance profile, and raster pipeline software tags.
7. **Photographic Evidence Exhibits**: High-resolution embedded visual panels:
   - *Exhibit A*: Questioned Ingested Item (Original Full-Frame Evidence)
   - *Exhibit B*: Biometric Facial Region of Interest
   - *Exhibit C*: 2D Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) Log-Magnitude Spectrum
   - *Exhibit D*: Grad-CAM Explainability Attention Heatmap
8. **Multi-Modal Forensic Findings & Spectral Analysis**: Generative AI prompt/model signature extraction, graphics editing tool detection, 2D FFT azimuthal periodicity, 2D DCT high-frequency energy ratio, and neural spatial-residual classification probabilities.
9. **Itemized Evidentiary Findings Ledger**: Structured table detailing finding categories, technical descriptions, legal/investigative severities, and risk score impacts.
10. **Forensic Expert Opinion & Certificate of Analysis**: Formal judicial conclusion formulated *"within a reasonable degree of scientific and forensic certainty"*, providing definitive determinations on authenticity, raster modification, or generative AI synthesis.
11. **Statutory Examiner Attestation & Sworn Declaration**: Formal declaration executed under penalty of perjury pursuant to 28 U.S.C. § 1746 and FRE 902(13)/(14); official Examiner Signature Block and Digital Verification Seal.
12. **Forensic Scope, Limitations & Procedural Notes**: Transparent delineation of performed checks, unavailable hardware examinations (e.g. PRNU sensor fingerprinting, C2PA credentials), and web compression caveats.

### Two-Pass Pagination & Layout Integrity:
Every page incorporates dynamic two-pass pagination (`Page X of Y`), running top headers with Case File Reference tokens, and running bottom footers citing confidentiality caveats and ISO/IEC 27037 compliance to prevent undetected page omission or alteration in legal proceedings.
